Videos

Two series, listed by session. Neither is required, and nothing in them is examinable unless it is also on the slides.

3B1B Essence of linear algebra, by Grant Sanderson (3Blue1Brown): short chapters, 10 to 20 minutes each, on the geometry behind the definitions. It is worth watching the series in order, chapters 1 to 16. It does not cover symmetric matrices or Markov chains.

MIT MIT 18.06 Linear Algebra, Spring 2010, by Gilbert Strang: full lectures, about 50 minutes each, with worked examples at the board. The notation differs from Lay in places.
