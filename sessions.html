Sessions

The eight sessions, with the sections of Lay they cover, their topics, suggested exercises from the book and videos. A topic links to its entry in the topic map.

Answers to the odd-numbered exercises are at the back of Lay.

SESSION 1

Linear equations in linear algebra

Lay 1.1 to 1.7

Three engineering problems that lead to a matrix equation. Systems of linear equations, row reduction and echelon forms, pivots, existence and uniqueness. Linear combinations and span. The matrix equation Ax = b and its solution sets. Linear independence.

Topics

1aRow reduction1bExistence, uniqueness1cSpan1dAx = b1eSolution sets1fLinear independence

Lay 1.8, 1.9, 2.1 to 2.3, 3.1 to 3.3

Linear transformations and their standard matrix. Matrix operations, the inverse of a matrix and the Invertible Matrix Theorem. Determinants: cofactor expansion, computation by Gaussian elimination, area and volume.

SESSION 3

Vector spaces, rank, image and kernel

Lay 2.8, 2.9, 4.1 to 4.6

Vector spaces and subspaces. The column space (the set of attainable outputs) and the null space (the set of inputs mapped to zero). Basis, coordinates and dimension. Rank and the Rank Theorem.
